# The tuning knobs, one line each: name | default | least | most | what it is.
# A default with a point in it makes a fraction (a DOUBLE in the code, name#); else a whole number.
# A line in [brackets] starts a group. tools/tuning/make_knobs.py writes io/tuning_knobs.bi,
# io/tuning_knobs.bm and scenarios/TUNING.DAT from this file. To add a knob: add its line here,
# run make_knobs.py, and use the variable in the code in place of the number.
# A knob's most must keep the INTEGER arithmetic it feeds below 32767, which QB64 wraps without an
# error: look at every expression that uses it. A saved game stores only the knobs off their
# defaults, so changing a default here changes the game of every saved game that kept it.

# Rules between knobs: check | a BASIC condition on the knobs' names | what a playtester is told.
# They are checked when every tuning file has been read (tuning_relations$).
check | huge_army_size > large_army_size | huge_army_size must be above large_army_size
check | cities_big_bonus_above > cities_bonus_above | cities_big_bonus_above must be above cities_bonus_above
check | new_army_supply + new_army_supply_spread + union_new_army_supply <= MAX_SUPPLY | new_army_supply, new_army_supply_spread and union_new_army_supply may add up to 10 at most
check | small_army_size < max_army_size | small_army_size must be below max_army_size
check | min_detach_size <= max_army_size | min_detach_size may not be above max_army_size
check | new_army_size <= max_army_size | new_army_size may not be above max_army_size
# A new army gets the Balance setting's extra troops for up to 2 steps (Balance 1 or 5) without
# the cap, and army_detach rounds the detached troops: a part of 0 troops would be an army.
check | new_army_size + 2 * balance_recruits <= max_army_size | new_army_size plus 2 times balance_recruits may not be above max_army_size
check | min_detach_size * detach_share >= 1 | min_detach_size times detach_share must be 1 or more
check | reinforce_size <= max_army_size | reinforce_size may not be above max_army_size
check | invasion_army_size <= max_army_size | invasion_army_size may not be above max_army_size
# The points: a side's points settle where a month's gain equals what points_kept takes away. The
# Union's income is at most capital_income, its bonus at Balance 5, and its cities: 590 in the
# shipped scenarios, plus 40 cities counted, rounded to 640. Both sides' points are added in an
# INTEGER (draw_top_bar), so each side stays below half of 32767, with room for one-time points.
# This bounds each month's gain only; points from events, captures and battles use the room left.
# The cash needs no check: 19999 plus at most 5000 + 5 * 1000 + 590 of income stays below 32767,
# with about 2000 left for the cash that events and the computer's ship refunds add in a month.
check | (points_per_income * (capital_income + 5 * union_bonus_per_balance + 640) + cities_bonus + cities_big_bonus) / (1 - points_kept) <= 15000 | a side's victory points may grow to 15000 at most: lower points_per_income, the bonuses or points_kept
check | ironclad_cost_times * cost_ship <= MAX_CASH | an ironclad (ironclad_cost_times times cost_ship) may cost 19999 at most
